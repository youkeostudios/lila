04 / Foundations

Identity

The logo’s purple movement and embedded play motif become the source for gradients, curved silhouettes and media interactions.

Background-aware logo library

11 production SVGs · transparent canvas

Primary lockup

Lila + Studios + tagline. Use for large identity moments where every line remains legible.

Studios lockup

Lila + Studios without the tagline. Use for credits, brand panels and medium-size placements.

Lila wordmark / compact logo

The stylised Lila artwork only. Use for navigation, footers, watermarks and compact controls.

Use explicit background-aware files in production. Do not invert a light-background logo with CSS filters, and do not extract the embedded play triangle as a standalone mark.
